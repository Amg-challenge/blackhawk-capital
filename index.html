Who we are

A multi-asset manager
built on discipline.

BlackHawk Capital is a multi-asset investment manager based in Lugano. We build globally diversified portfolios across equities, government and corporate bonds, gold and commodities, combining a clear view of the economic cycle with a rigorous framework for risk.

Our mission

To grow our investors’ capital steadily through the cycle, taking part in global growth without letting any single market, rate path or asset class decide the outcome.
01

Risk comes first

We set the risk budget before we choose the weights. Every position earns its place by the share of risk it brings, not by the capital it takes.

02

Genuine diversification

Equities, duration, credit and real assets respond to different economic forces. We combine them so that no single engine drives the portfolio.

03

Full transparency

Every decision is documented, tested and reproducible. Our investors can see what we expected, what happened and what we changed as a result.

Our approach

Risk decides the weights.
Not capital.

We do not try to forecast which asset class wins next. Our USD 100 million simulated mandate earns from several independent sources of return, and every position is sized by the share of portfolio risk it brings, not by the capital it uses.

How we size each position

Capital weight is not risk weight.

Eight broad ETFs are weighted by capped Equal Risk Contribution: each position is sized to contribute a similar share of portfolio volatility, while concentration limits keep any single exposure in check.

Why this method

A rule we can explain, test and defend.

Principle 1

No return forecasts

The weights depend on volatilities and correlations, not on expected returns. For broad asset classes, future returns are the input we can estimate least reliably.

Principle 2

Correlations count

Unlike simple risk parity, the method uses the full covariance matrix, so funds that tend to fall together are not treated as independent sources of risk.

Principle 3

Caps against estimation error

Risk-based rules favour whatever looked quietest in the sample. Short credit looked unusually calm during the rate shock, so we cap it instead of letting one quiet fund absorb the portfolio.

We compared equal weight, risk parity, uncapped ERC, minimum variance and maximum Sharpe. Capped ERC is a choice of process and portfolio structure: it is not the method with the highest historical Sharpe, and our report shows that trade-off openly.

Focused stock research

A quantitative perspective

A factor strategy and a macroeconomic view, added within a tracking-error budget and always assessed against the strategic core.
