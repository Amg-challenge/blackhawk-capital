Black Hawk Capital · USI Lugano
01
Approach

One strategic core, three disciplined steps.

We combine geographic diversification and investment style within equities with Treasury duration, short corporate credit, bullion and commodity futures. We set portfolio weights from their joint risk, while recognizing that correlations can change.

I

Strategic Asset Allocation

Eight broad ETFs weighted by Capped Equal Risk Contribution. Formation risk uses 60 complete monthly USD returns; the 8% target allows an ex-ante volatility range of 7–9%.

Portfolio

Capital weight is not risk weight.

We target balanced contributions to portfolio risk, subject to concentration limits. Our capital caps are 12% in IGSB and 15% each in GLD and DBC; the chosen allocation also passes the 50% equity ceiling and the 7–9% ex-ante volatility check.
